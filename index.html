PROJECT FILE
OPERATOR QUICK START

Your first live set.

Press play. Five original, synthesized demo sketches are already in the instrument: drum & bass, gangsta house, midtempo, 140 and UK garage. They are not commercial recordings. Choose a genre to start its loop. Audio starts after a click; master volume begins low.

Perform the image. Choose a 3D source, move the sliders, blend in a real project, or hold M for a datamosh-style smear. Feedback is real temporal frame history. Pixel sorting reorders luminance within 16-pixel blocks. These are original procedural visuals, not copies of Unit44 footage.

Choose an input. Local audio and microphone are analyzed in your browser; no upload or recording occurs. Tap T for manual tempo. There is no claim of automatic DJ beat matching for outside audio. Mic is never sent to your speakers.

Keep control. Space starts/pauses the session, 1–4 switch scenes, and R resets effects. The visual pause is independent of music. Reduced-motion preferences are respected until you explicitly enable visual motion. Pausing or leaving the page stops live microphone tracks.

Spotify is separate. Paste a playlist, album or track into the Spotify listening room. It pauses the VJ engine and other audio; there is no Spotify analysis or visual sync. Spotify controls account access and available playback.

No strobe effect is included, but glitch effects and rapid camera motion can still be uncomfortable. Pause visuals or browse the portfolio at any time.
