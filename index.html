Technical direction, from plan to show.

Technical Director and Production Manager with over a decade in lighting, video and carpentry for live events and immersive exhibitions. My highlights include serving as Technical Director and Department Head at Republic Club in Pattaya, coordinating video, lighting, lasers and kinetic systems, and developing projection and lighting concepts for TheXperience in Koh Samui. I connect creative ideas with the crews, systems and hands-on fabrication needed to deliver them.

OPERATOR QUICK START

Mix visuals in your browser.

Choose a generated source, add a stinger, or load your own visual. The lab starts in silence.

Your soundtrack. Load a local audio file or enable the microphone. Audio stays in your browser. Microphone input is analyzed without being sent to the speakers.

Motion and tempo. Use Visuals to pause or resume motion. Set BPM or tap T to establish a manual clock. Frequency energy drives the audio response; the lab does not automatically identify a track’s tempo.

Reset. Reset all effects restores a clean image. Close the lab to stop audio and release microphone access. Keyboard shortcuts work only while the lab is open.

Spotify opens in a separate player and does not feed the effects.
